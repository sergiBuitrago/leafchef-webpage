A few good questions

Before you
take a look.

Is LeafChef only for vegan meals?+

Yes. Vegan cooking is at the heart of LeafChef. It’s made for vegan households and people who want to become fully vegan, with vegan meal planning, pantry management, and shared shopping in one place.

Can LeafChef help me become fully vegan?+

LeafChef helps with the everyday planning: deciding what vegan meals to cook, using ingredients you have, and knowing what to buy. Start with the days and meals you want planned, find dishes you enjoy, and build a vegan cooking routine at your own pace.

Can I choose when I want to cook?+

Yes. In Weekly meals, select the days and meal types you want LeafChef to plan: breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a snack. You can keep your usual weekly routine as the starting point for future plans and leave other meals unselected.

Does it use AI to plan meals?+

LeafChef uses AI to suggest recipes and weekly meal drafts based on your household’s preferences. You review the draft and can replace individual meals before applying the plan.

Can I use it with my household?+

Yes. Household members share a pantry, shopping list, and meal plan. LeafChef is currently being tested in everyday use on two Android phones.

Can I download it or use it in a browser?+

LeafChef is currently an Android app in private household testing. There’s no public download or browser version yet. This website introduces the app; it doesn’t connect to your kitchen data.
